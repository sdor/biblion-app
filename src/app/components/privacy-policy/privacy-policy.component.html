Privacy Policy

Biblion by Biosys Technologies

Biblion ("the Add-in", "the Service") is a Microsoft Word Add-in developed and operated by Biosys Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information with transparency.

1 Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Account credentials — if you choose to create a Biblion account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. Account creation is entirely optional; the core search and citation features work without an account.
  • Display name — an optional name you may set for your profile.

1.2 Information Generated by Your Use of the Service

  • Search queries — search terms you type are sent to the NCBI Entrez E-utilities API (operated by the National Library of Medicine) to retrieve PubMed article results. These queries are transmitted directly to NCBI and are subject to NLM's privacy policy .
  • Saved bibliography items — if you are signed in, article metadata you save (PubMed IDs, titles, authors, journal information, abstracts) is stored on Biosys Technologies servers to enable cloud library sync across devices.
  • Session tokens — a JSON Web Token (JWT) is stored in your browser's localStorage to maintain your authenticated session. This token is not transmitted to any third party.

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We never access or upload the contents of your Word documents. Biblion reads only cursor position and content control metadata within Word to manage in-document citations. No document text, file contents, or manuscript data is transmitted to Biosys Technologies servers or any third party.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or usage analytics.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party.

2 How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and maintain your session securely.
  • To sync your saved bibliography library across devices when you are signed in.
  • To respond to support requests you send to us by email.
  • To send transactional emails (e.g., password reset), if requested.
  • To improve the reliability and performance of the Service (aggregate, non-identifiable usage patterns only).

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

3 Data Storage and Security

Biblion API data is hosted on servers located in the United States. We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption in transit, bcrypt password hashing, and JWT-based session management. While no system is perfectly secure, we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss.

4 Data Retention

Your account data and saved bibliography items are retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

5 Third-Party Services

6 Children's Privacy

Biblion is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7 Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@biosystechnologies.com.
