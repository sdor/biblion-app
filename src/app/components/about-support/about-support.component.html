Getting Started with Biblion

Search PubMed

Search over 36 million biomedical citations. Enter keywords, topic terms, PMIDs, or author names (e.g. Doudna J[Author]).

2

Insert In-Text Citation

Click your cursor where you want a citation in Word and click Insert Citation. Biblion inserts a native Content Control in your chosen format.

3

Automatic References

Biblion automatically appends and synchronizes a structured References bibliography list at the end of the document.

4

Switch Citation Styles

Switch effortlessly between APA 7th, IEEE, Nature, AMA, Harvard, and Vancouver. All citations update immediately.

5

Live Cursor Tracking

As you write and move your cursor across your document, the Live References tab automatically detects citations at your cursor position in real time. Live Cursor Tracking is available exclusively for signed-in users.

Personal Library & Sync

Save important articles to your session library. Because local storage is erased when you close your window or tab, you can use Export Bibliography and Load Bibliography to save and restore your references. Synchronization is supported only when you are signed in on your account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account or pay to use Biblion?

No. Biblion is completely free for literature discovery, PubMed abstract viewing, in-text citation inserting, and bibliography generation. Account registration is free and unlocks advanced workflow capabilities: Live Cursor Tracking (real-time sentence-level citation inspection and one-click citation removal in Microsoft Word) and cloud library synchronization across all your devices and sessions. Live Cursor Tracking and cloud synchronization are supported only when you are signed in on your account.

How do I remove a citation or remove a particular citation from text?

The easiest, most reliable way to remove or edit citations is using Biblion's Live Cursor Tracking in the Live References tab:

  1. Position Your Cursor: Click anywhere inside or adjacent to the sentence or citation you want to remove in Microsoft Word.
  2. Open Live References: The Live References tab in the Biblion sidepane immediately detects and displays all references cited at that sentence or paragraph in real time.
  3. Click Remove: Each detected reference card displays a red Remove button. Clicking it removes that specific citation from your document text immediately.

Why Live Cursor Tracking is vital for citations management:

  • Precision with Multi-Citation Groups: When multiple papers are cited together (for example, (Wu et al., 2020; Doudna, 2012) or [1-3]), manually deleting text inside Word can corrupt the underlying Word Content Control or delete all citations at once. Live Cursor Tracking allows you to selectively remove a single reference while keeping all other citations in the group intact and properly formatted.
  • Automatic Document Re-Indexing: When you remove a citation via Live Cursor Tracking, Biblion instantly recalculates citation order and numbering across the entire document (in numbered styles like Nature, AMA, Vancouver, and IEEE) and updates the structured References bibliography list at the end of the document.
  • Contextual Verification: You can read the full PubMed abstract, author lists, and publication details in the Live References pane before confirming removal, ensuring you never remove the wrong reference by accident.

Note: If you manually highlight and delete the entire citation content control in Word with your keyboard (Backspace/Delete), Biblion will synchronize the document the next time you insert or format citations. However, using Live Cursor Tracking is strongly recommended to guarantee document XML integrity and prevent citation desynchronization. Live Cursor Tracking is available exclusively for signed-in users.

Why is My Library erased when I close my window/tab, and how do I restore it?

When using Biblion without signing in, My Library operates as a privacy-friendly, session-based workspace in local storage. Whenever you close the window or tab, the contents of My Library are automatically erased.

The way to restore the content of My Library across sessions as a guest is to click Export Bibliography (JSON format) before closing your window or tab, and click Load Bibliography to load it again when you return.

Synchronization is supported only when you are signed in on your account. When signed in, all saved references, personal notes, tags, and collections are automatically synchronized to the cloud and restored seamlessly whenever you open Biblion.

What should I do if the "Insert Citation" button does not appear?

The Insert Citation button appears when Biblion is running as an Add-in inside Microsoft Word. When accessed in a standalone browser, Biblion provides a convenient Copy Citation button instead.

How does Biblion handle my document's privacy and data?

Biblion operates locally within your Word taskpane. Search queries are sent securely over HTTPS directly to the official NCBI Entrez E-utilities API. Biblion does not upload, inspect, or store your document text on external servers.
